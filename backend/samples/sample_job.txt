Junior Data Scientist — Northwind Analytics (Hybrid, Manchester)

About us
Northwind Analytics helps mid-sized retailers make better decisions with data. Our small, friendly
team builds forecasting and customer analytics products used by 60+ retail brands.

The role
We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist to join our Customer Insights squad. You will build models
that predict customer behaviour, run experiments, and explain results to clients in plain English.

What you will do
- Build and evaluate machine learning models (e.g. churn, customer lifetime value, demand forecasting).
- Design and analyse A/B tests for client marketing campaigns.
- Write clean, tested Python code and SQL that runs in production pipelines.
- Present findings to non-technical clients through dashboards and short reports.
- Work with data engineers to deploy models using Docker and AWS.

What we are looking for (required)
- 2+ years of experience working with data in a professional setting.
- Strong Python (pandas, scikit-learn) and SQL.
- Understanding of core statistics: hypothesis testing, regression, confidence intervals.
- Experience building at least one predictive model end to end.
- Clear communication with non-technical stakeholders.

Nice to have
- A/B testing / experimentation experience.
- Docker, AWS (S3, SageMaker) or another cloud platform.
- Experience with dbt or Airflow.
- Retail or e-commerce domain knowledge.
- Power BI or Tableau.

What we offer
£38,000–£45,000, hybrid working (2 days in office), learning budget, 27 days holiday.
